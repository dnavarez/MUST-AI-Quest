## Copied from the quest page

### Unlearn, Use, Raise the Load

TIME REMAINING (when the screenshots were taken): 2d 12h 51m

**OBJECTIVE**

Build one small tool in 5 days, working AI-first. We want to see how
much you change the way you work, not how much you already know.

**FORMAT**

- A: Inbox helper. Takes a pile of candidate messages, groups them by
  what the person wants, and drafts a short reply for each. A human
  reviews every draft before it is sent.
- B: Attendance checker. Reads a CSV of work hours and leave, flags
  unusual patterns, and explains each flag in one sentence. Make your
  own sample data.
- C: Stall alerter. Reads a CSV of a hiring board, finds candidates who
  have not moved for a set number of days, and lists who to follow up
  first and what to do.

### Deliverables

1. Loom video
2. intent.md
3. Final directive.md
4. Working code, with a README that says how to run it.
5. A short log for each of the 5 days.
6. A 5-minute video showing it working.
7. A one-page note on what you unlearned.

### How your submission is evaluated

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Unlearning : the habits you dropped show up in the work and the log. | 30% |
| AI usage : you prompt well, check the results, and iterate. | 25% |
| Raising the load : you really made it harder, and you report honestly what broke. | 20% |
| Quality : it runs, it has tests, and the code is readable. | 15% |
| Honesty : you say what failed and do not hide it. | 10% |
